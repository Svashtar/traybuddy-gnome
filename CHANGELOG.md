## [Unreleased]

### Added
- First version in development: choose for each top-bar icon whether it is **Shown**, **Collapsible** (behind an arrow) or **Hidden**.
- Drag icons into your own order in the settings, with a live preview of the top bar.
- The arrow slides the collapsible icons out in a pill and turns to show it can close again.
- Collapsible icons collapse again after a set number of seconds without use; the timer waits while the pointer is on them or one of their menus is open.
- Optional: open on hover, a choice of pill background (none, subtle or accent colour) and Keep Open from the arrow's right-click menu.
- Choose whether the arrow sits right of the shown icons, next to the system menu (the default), or left of them.
- Remembers icons of apps that aren't running, so they can be set up in advance or forgotten.
- About page with release notes, debug info for bug reports and settings backup.
